Questions

Straight Answers

No pressure and no jargon.

Is there really no obligation?

Correct. Requesting an offer is free and confidential. You can say no, and you will not hear any pressure from us.

Do I need to make repairs or clean the house?

No. We buy as-is, in any condition. Leave what you do not want to take with you.

Are there fees or commissions?

There are no agent commissions, no closing costs and no title fees taken out of our offer. The money we offer is the money you walk away with. Terms and conditions apply.

How fast can you close?

As soon as 7 days. If you need more time to move, you choose the closing date.

What if my house is listed with a realtor?

We can only buy properties that are not currently listed. If your listing expires or you take it off the market, reach out and we will be glad to help.

What if I am not the owner?

We work with people who have the legal right to sell. If you hold power of attorney or are an executor or court-appointed representative, you can still request an offer.

What areas do you buy in?

We buy houses throughout Maryland.
